OneWord Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026.

Your reading data

Books, documents, progress, stats, summaries, flashcards and settings are stored only on your device. OneWord has no user accounts or servers of its own, and does not collect this data. Summaries, quizzes, OCR and reading are processed on-device; translation uses Apple's translation feature.

Ads

The app shows a small ad banner provided by Google AdMob in the Library and Stats. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location and ad usage and performance data. You choose whether to allow tracking in the iOS prompt and, in regions such as the European Economic Area and the UK, in the consent form. You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and, where available, in “Ads & privacy” > “Ad privacy options”. More information: how Google uses data in ads.

Permissions

Camera: scan book pages (OCR) and, optionally, detect distraction or eye fatigue. Processing is local; no image or facial data is recorded or transmitted. Photos: select document images to convert into text.

Web articles

When you import an article by link, the app downloads the page you specified directly from its source site.

Children

OneWord is not directed at children under 13.
