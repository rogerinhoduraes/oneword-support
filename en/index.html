Frequently asked questions

Is the app really free?
Yes. All features are free. The app shows a small ad banner in the Library and Stats (never in the reader). A one-time purchase removes ads.

How do I remove ads or restore my purchase?
Open the + menu in the Library and tap “Remove ads”. “Restore Purchases” is there too.

Which formats can I import?
EPUB, PDF, gallery images, pages scanned with the camera and web articles.

Are my books and reading sent to a server?
No. Your books, progress and stats stay on your device. See the privacy policy.
